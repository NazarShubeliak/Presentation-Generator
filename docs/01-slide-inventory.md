## Cross-deck observations (descriptive, for WP3 follow-up)

- A handful of slide types are **shared verbatim (or near-verbatim) across all 5 decks**: the service/value-prop bullets, the "Jeder Markt bekommt seine eigene Welt" portfolio grid, the "Best-Seller der Saison 2025" generic motif table, the social-reach/stats layout, and the contact slide.
- The number of "own-market" motif-table slides **varies per deck**: 2 slides / 3 motifs (Basel, Erzgebirgsdorf), 2 slides / 5 motifs (Freiburg), 6 slides / 6 theme worlds (Magdeburg), 5 slides / 5 theme worlds (Halle). WP3's page-type catalogue needs to treat this as a variable-length repeating block, not a fixed count.
- One deck (**Erzgebirgsdorf**) reuses a stock kiosk-screenshot photo showing **Duisburg** market backgrounds on the "user flow" slide, mismatched to its own market — plus a motif table subheading ("Märchenwelt Schwerin") and a transition-slide branding ("Erzgebirgsdorf Chemnitz") that don't match its own title market (Düsseldorfer Platz). Flagging this for Martin — looks like incomplete re-theming from copy-pasted source decks, not something to replicate in the generator. (Magdeburg had the same Duisburg-kiosk-photo issue in an outdated file reviewed earlier — the current file, replaced 2026-08-17, has this fixed; Erzgebirgsdorf's file has not been re-checked for a similar update.)
- Two decks are structurally elaborate outliers, both built around several distinct "theme worlds" rather than one hero booth + a couple of motif tables: **Halle** (5 theme worlds, 5 motif tables — 4 of the 5 also get a dedicated booth photo, shown solo, paired, or folded into the service slide) and **Magdeburg** (6 theme worlds, 6 motif tables — 2 absorbed into title/service booth photos, 4 additionally get a 2-up theme-showcase slide). The master template/schema (later WPs) will need to support this as an optional repeating structure, not assume every location has exactly one theme or a fixed 1:1 mapping between booth photos and motif tables.
- Not every theme-world motif is location-specific even in these elaborate decks: Magdeburg's "Märchengasse" (fairy-tale characters), "Weihnachtsmannhaus & Kinderdorf" (Santa/elves), and "Magdeburger Lichterwelt" (generic winter/snow figures) themes read as generic/universal, same as Halle's "Märchen- und Familienwelt" — while "Nordische Meile" (Viking/Sámi motifs) is distinctive but not obviously Magdeburg-specific either. Only "Kaiser-Otto-Pfalz" and "Alter Markt & Rathaus" tie to real Magdeburg history/landmarks. Worth confirming with the designer whether generic theme worlds like these are meant to be reusable/optional building blocks in the schema, distinct from the strictly-local ones.
