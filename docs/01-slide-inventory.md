## Cross-deck observations (descriptive, for WP3 follow-up)

- A handful of slide types are **shared verbatim (or near-verbatim) across all 5 decks**: the service/value-prop bullets, the "Jeder Markt bekommt seine eigene Welt" portfolio grid, the "Best-Seller der Saison 2025" generic motif table, the social-reach/stats layout, and the contact slide.
- The number of "own-market" motif-table slides **varies per deck**: 2 slides / 3 motifs (Basel, Erzgebirgsdorf), 2 slides / 5 motifs (Freiburg), 2 slides / 6 motifs (Magdeburg), 5 slides / 15 motifs across 5 theme worlds (Halle). WP3's page-type catalogue needs to treat this as a variable-length repeating block, not a fixed count.
- Two decks (**Erzgebirgsdorf**, **Magdeburg**) reuse a stock kiosk-screenshot photo showing **Duisburg** market backgrounds on the "user flow" slide, mismatched to their own market. The Erzgebirgsdorf deck additionally has a motif table subheading ("Märchenwelt Schwerin") and a transition-slide branding ("Erzgebirgsdorf Chemnitz") that don't match its own title market (Düsseldorfer Platz). Flagging this for Martin — looks like incomplete re-theming from copy-pasted source decks, not something to replicate in the generator.
- The Halle deck is structurally the outlier: instead of one hero booth + a couple of motif tables, it presents 5 distinct "theme worlds," each with its own motif table (4 of the 5 also get a dedicated booth photo — shown solo, paired, or folded into the service slide; "Märchen- und Familienwelt" has a motif table but no dedicated booth photo). The master template/schema (later WPs) will need to support this as an optional repeating structure, not assume every location has exactly one theme or a fixed 1:1 mapping between booth photos and motif tables.
